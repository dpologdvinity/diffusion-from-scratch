What the experiments show

FID is measured on 1,000 samples per setting in a small classifier's feature space (lower is better; real images score about 3), so it compares settings within this project, not with published numbers.

  • DDIM matches 1000-step DDPM with 20× fewer steps. On MNIST at guidance 2, 50-step DDIM reaches the same class accuracy (99.9% vs 99.9%) at a lower FID (19.0 vs 28.0), and FashionMNIST agrees (FID 13.8 vs 24.3).
  • The gap comes from DDPM's injected noise, not its step count. Turning DDIM's noise (η) up from 0 to 1 at 50 steps moves FID from 19.0 to 30.1, close to DDPM.
  • Guidance trades variety for fidelity. Guidance 1 gives the best FID (8.5) at 92.7% accuracy; guidance 2 reaches 99.9% accuracy at FID 19.0.
  • A cosine noise schedule helps guided sampling but hurts unguided sampling. Against a linear model trained identically, it cuts 20-step DDIM FID at guidance 2 from 17.2 to 9.5, but at guidance 1 FID rises from 9.5 to 18.8.

Full tables, the FashionMNIST results, and the schedule ablation are in the README; the math behind each sampler is in NOTES.md.
